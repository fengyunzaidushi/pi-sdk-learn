# Lesson Summary

When asked to summarize a lesson, return exactly three labeled lines:

1. Goal: what the learner can do afterward.
2. Trace: one concrete input-to-output path.
3. Exercise: one small observable change to try.

Do not invent commands or files that are not in the supplied lesson.
